Questions.

Does it record what I type?

No. KEY VIBES listens to keyboard events only to play a sound. It doesn't record, store or send what you type, and it has no analytics or logs.

Does it need the internet?

No. Sounds play offline. It checks for updates about once a day (you can turn that off). If you activate a licence key, it also confirms the key about once a week, and keeps working for 21 days if you're offline.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, and Windows warns about new, unsigned apps. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Does it work on Ubuntu with Wayland?

Yes. Wayland hides keystrokes from other apps, so KEY VIBES shows a Grant access button. It asks for your password once, then hears every keyboard, including ones you plug in later.

Can I use it on a new computer?

Your key works on two computers. To move one, choose Deactivate this computer in the app, then activate on the new one.

What do I get after the trial if I don't buy?

Linear Red, Buckling Spring and Wood Block keep working. Sound packs you add yourself are always free.
